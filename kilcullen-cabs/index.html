How booking works

Three steps. No surprises.

  1. Send your trip

    Use the booking form, WhatsApp or a phone call. Tell us your pickup, destination and time.

  2. We confirm your price

    We reply with your fare and pickup details. Nothing is booked until you've agreed the price.

  3. Your driver arrives

    On time, at your door, with their driver ID on display in the car.

Every taxi and hackney driver in Ireland must hold a licence from the National Transport Authority. You can check any driver or vehicle with the NTA's free Driver Check app.

Questions

Before you book

Are you open every day?

Yes — Kilcullen Cabs runs 7 days a week. Call 089 245 8888 or 045 483 333, or send your booking with the form at the top of this page.

How do I book a cab?

Fill in the booking form at the top of this page, send us a WhatsApp message or call. Your booking is confirmed once we reply with your price and pickup time.

How far ahead should I book?

For airport runs and race days, book at least a day ahead — those are our busiest trips. For local journeys, a few hours' notice is usually enough, but it's always worth asking if you need a cab sooner.

Do you do school runs?

Yes — a dependable lift to and from the school gates. Tell us the school, the days and the times when you book, and we'll set up a regular run.

How much will my journey cost?

The booking form shows a guide price for popular destinations, based on the NTA's maximum fares and including the €3 booking fee. We confirm the exact price before you travel, and it will never be more than the NTA maximum.

Can you collect me from Dublin Airport?

Yes. Add your flight number when you book so we can time the pickup to your landing, and tell us which terminal you're arriving at.

How can I pay?

Tell us how you'd like to pay when you book and we'll confirm the options for your trip.

Do you have child seats, wheelchair-accessible cars or room for extra luggage?

Add what you need in the notes when you book and we'll confirm what's available before your trip.

What if my plans change?

Message or call us as soon as you can and we'll change or cancel your booking.
